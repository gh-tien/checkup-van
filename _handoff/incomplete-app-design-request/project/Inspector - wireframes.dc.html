1Inspector module — home screen directions

Three takes on what an inspector sees when they open the app. The job is narrow — walk a van, log faults — so every direction leads with the one action (start a check) and differs in what surrounds it: a calm action-first card, a progress/streak dashboard, or a prioritised worklist. Same nav on all three (Check · Defects · Fleet · Approvals · More). Pick a direction and I'll build it into the real app.
